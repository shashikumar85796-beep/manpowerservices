Questions
Things clients ask first.

    

      
Who pays the workers?
We do. Workers are on our payroll, and we file PF, ESI and other statutory dues. You pay us one monthly invoice based on attendance.

      
What if a worker leaves or underperforms?
We replace the worker within 24 hours at no extra fee. Repeat issues go to your account manager for review.

      
Can we hire a worker permanently later?
Yes. After an agreed contract period, you can convert any worker to your own payroll. Terms are fixed in the agreement up front.

      
How do you price a crew?
Wages and statutory costs at actuals, plus a transparent service fee per worker. We share the full breakup before you sign.

      
Do you cover small requirements?
Yes. We supply anything from 5 workers to several hundred. Start small and scale as the site grows.
